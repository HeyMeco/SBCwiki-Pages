## Summary

Imagination Technologies is a British semiconductor IP company headquartered in Kings Langley, England. It licenses its PowerVR GPU designs (A-, B-, C-, D- and E-Series, and the older Rogue Series 6/7/8) to SoC vendors such as Texas Instruments, Allwinner, StarFive, T-Head and MediaTek, and is the most common GPU IP in RISC-V SoCs.
