## Summary

Allwinner Technology is a Chinese fabless semiconductor company headquartered in Zhuhai, Guangdong. Founded in 2007, the company specializes in designing systems-on-chip (SoCs) and integrated circuits. Their processors are widely used in Android-based tablets, smartphones, OTT boxes, camera systems, and other smart devices.
