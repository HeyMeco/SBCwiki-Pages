# Libre Computer Tritium (ALL-H3-CC)

Tritium is Libre Computer's Allwinner board in the Raspberry Pi 3 B+ footprint, funded on Kickstarter in January 2018. It comes in three versions with pin-compatible SoCs: the **H2+ with 512 MB** (IoT, 1080p HDMI), the **H3 with 1 GB** and the 64-bit **H5 with 2 GB**. Its four USB 2.0 ports each have their own SoC channel instead of sharing a hub. Libre Computer supports it with mainline Linux until 2047.
