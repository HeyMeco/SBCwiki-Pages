# NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor Developer Kit

The AGX Thor Developer Kit pairs the Jetson T5000 module with NVIDIA's reference carrier, a 1 TB NVMe SSD, a Wi-Fi 6E module and a 140 W power supply. Cameras connect through Holoscan Sensor Bridge over the QSFP28 port, or over USB. It launched at $3,499 in August 2025. CNX Software reported that NVIDIA's July 2026 price list raised it to $5,499.
