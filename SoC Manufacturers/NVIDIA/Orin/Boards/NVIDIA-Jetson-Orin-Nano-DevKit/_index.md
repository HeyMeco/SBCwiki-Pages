# NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super Developer Kit

The Jetson Orin Nano Developer Kit launched in March 2023 at $499. In December 2024 NVIDIA renamed it the **Super** Developer Kit and cut the price to $249. The hardware is unchanged: JetPack 6.1 adds the Super power modes, which raise performance to 67 TOPS. CNX Software reported that NVIDIA's July 2026 price list raised it to $399. The P3768 carrier also accepts Orin NX modules. The Orin Nano 2, announced in August 2026, is due in the first half of 2027.
