# NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin Developer Kit

The AGX Orin Developer Kit comes in a 110 × 110 mm enclosure with the module, heatsink and fan. It launched in March 2022 with the 32 GB module and was later sold with the 64 GB module. Its PCIe x16 slot is wired as Gen4 x8. It launched at $1,999. CNX Software reported that NVIDIA's July 2026 price list raised it to $3,499.
