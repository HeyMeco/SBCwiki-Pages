## Summary

Qualcomm is an American multinational corporation headquartered in San Diego, California, and incorporated in Delaware that creates semiconductors, software and services related to wireless technology. It owns patents critical to the mobile communications standards and has developed their own Oryon ARM CPU cores as well as licenses Cortex-A designs from [ARM](/docs/soc-manufacturers/arm/) which get rebranded as Kryo cores. Their GPU Line is called Adreno.
