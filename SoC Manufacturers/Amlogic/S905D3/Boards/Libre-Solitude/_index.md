# Libre Computer Solitude (AML-S905D3-CC)

Solitude is Libre Computer's S905D3 board in the CC (Raspberry Pi 3 B+) form factor. It was announced in September 2023 and shipped from December 2023. It is designed to run upstream distributions out of the box through UEFI. Mainline Linux has supported it since 6.7, and support runs until 2053.
