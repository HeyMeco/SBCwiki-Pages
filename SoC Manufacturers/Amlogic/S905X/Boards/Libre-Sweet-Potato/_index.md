# Libre Computer Sweet Potato (AML-S905X-CC-V2)

Sweet Potato is the updated [Le Potato](../libre-le-potato/), on sale since September 2023. It switches to DDR4 and USB-C power, adds UEFI firmware in 16 MB of SPI NOR flash, a screw-mount eMMC connector and a PoE header. It can install Linux images directly from the internet. Support runs until 2053.
