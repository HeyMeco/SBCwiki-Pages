# SiEngine

![SiEngine Logo](assets/siengine/SiEngine-Logo.png?h=80&f=webp)

SiEngine Technology (芯擎科技) is a Wuhan-based chip designer best known for automotive cockpit SoCs. Its first 7 nm chip, the SE1000, is also sold as the SE1000-I for industrial AIoT. Radxa uses it on the SiRider S1.
