# Texas Instruments

![Texas Instruments Logo](assets/ti/TexasInstruments-Logo.png?h=90&f=webp)

Texas Instruments makes the Sitara and Jacinto Arm processors for industrial and automotive markets. Its AM6xA vision processors combine Cortex-A72 cores with C7x/MMA deep-learning accelerators, an Imagination BXS-4-64 GPU and a vision ISP. TI sells them on low-cost SK starter kits.
