# BananaPi R4 Lite

The BPI-R4 Lite is the cheaper MT7987A sibling of the [BananaPi R4](/docs/soc-manufacturers/mediatek/filogic-880/boards/bananapi-r4/), with the same 148 × 100.5 mm footprint. Banana Pi markets it as a Filogic 850-class design. It drops the 10G SFP+ ports and the NVMe slot, but keeps Wi-Fi 7 card slots and adds an M.2 B-Key slot for 4G/5G modems.
