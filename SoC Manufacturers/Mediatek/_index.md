## Summary

MediaTek designs application and networking processors from Hsinchu, Taiwan. For IoT and single-board computers the current brand is **Genio**, including the flagship **Genio Pro** series. Chromebook and laptop parts use **Kompanio**. Router and access-point parts use **Filogic**.

Some Chromebook SKUs are the same silicon as Genio parts under a different brand, such as Genio Pro 5100 and Kompanio Ultra 910. Filogic parts use **MT79xx** numbers. CPUs are Arm Cortex designs. GPUs are Arm Mali or Immortalis. On-device AI uses MediaTek NPUs or Vision DSPs.

Collabora and other partners have been upstreaming Genio and Kompanio support into mainline Linux. Filogic is widely used in OpenWrt.

## Genio

Genio is MediaTek's IoT and edge AI brand. Longevity windows are typically about ten years from introduction.

## Filogic

Filogic is MediaTek's Wi-Fi router and gateway brand. OpenWrt targets these parts under the `mediatek/filogic` platform.
