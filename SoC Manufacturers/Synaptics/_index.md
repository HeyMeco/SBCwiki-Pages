# Synaptics

![Synaptics Logo](assets/synaptics/Synaptics-Logo.png?h=80&f=webp)

Synaptics, best known for touch and display controllers, sells Arm-based edge AI processors under the **Astra** brand. The SL-series (SL1620, SL1640, SL1680) are Linux-capable SoCs with NPUs. Since October 2025 Toradex supports them with Torizon.
