# Libre Computer Renegade Elite (ROC-RK3399-PC)

Renegade Elite is Libre Computer's RK3399 board, built with Firefly and crowdfunded on Indiegogo in July 2018. Its low-profile 120 × 72 mm "PC" form factor has two USB 3.0 Type-C ports with DisplayPort, one of which takes Power Delivery input. It also has eMMC, an RTC and UEFI firmware in SPI NOR. Mainline Linux and U-Boot support it until 2048.
