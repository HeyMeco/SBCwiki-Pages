# Seeed Studio reComputer Industrial RK3576

The reComputer Industrial RK3576 (D5-0864) is Seeed's fanless aluminium RK3576 box for DIN-rail or wall mounting. Next to the usual edge AI I/O it adds isolated industrial interfaces (2× RS-485, CAN FD, a digital input and a digital output) and a 9–36 V terminal-block power input. Like the other reComputer RK3576 systems it ships with Armbian.
