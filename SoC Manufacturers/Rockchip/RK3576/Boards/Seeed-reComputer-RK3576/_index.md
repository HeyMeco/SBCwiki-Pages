# Seeed Studio reComputer RK3576

The reComputer RK3576 is Seeed Studio's enclosed RK3576 edge AI box. It shares its 123 × 93 × 51 mm chassis and most of its I/O with the [reComputer RK3588](/docs/soc-manufacturers/rockchip/rk3588/boards/seeed-recomputer-rk3588/). It ships with Armbian and Seeed's reComputer AI Lab for one-click model deployment, and its M.2 slot can take an RK1820 accelerator for up to 26 TOPS. The standard SKUs are the **RK3576-20** (4 GB) and **RK3576-30** (8 GB), and 16 GB is available to order. It launched in May 2026 from $99, and pre-orders shipped from 30 May 2026.
