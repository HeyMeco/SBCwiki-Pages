# Seeed Studio reComputer RK3576 Dev Kit

The reComputer RK3576 Dev Kit pairs Seeed's **reComputer RK3576 Module** with its **IO Board**. The module uses a 200-pin board-to-board connector, so you can design it into your own product. The module and IO board are also sold separately. A second kit adds the **RK1820 AI accelerator**: 20 TOPS INT8 with 2.5 GB of on-chip DRAM, for 26 TOPS in total. The RK1820 takes the only M.2 slot, so that kit has no room for an NVMe SSD. Both kits launched in September 2026, at $219 for the standard kit and $379 with the RK1820. They include a 12 V / 3 A adapter, a 32 GB microSD card, active cooling and antennas.
