# Firefly ICORE-3572Q38

The ICORE-3572Q38 is Firefly's 38 × 38 mm RK3572 system-on-module, with two 100-pin board-to-board sockets. Firefly lists Debian and Ubuntu images and the RKNN toolchain for the NPU.
