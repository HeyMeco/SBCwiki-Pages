# Orange Pi 5

The Orange Pi 5 was one of the first low-cost RK3588S boards, starting at $60 at launch. Its 100 × 62 mm board has M.2 NVMe, HDMI 2.1 and DisplayPort over USB-C. It has no onboard Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi comes from an optional M.2 module that takes the NVMe slot.
