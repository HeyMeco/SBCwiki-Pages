# Seeed Studio reComputer RK3588

The reComputer RK3588 is Seeed Studio's enclosed RK3588 edge AI box. It uses the same chassis as the [reComputer RK3576](/docs/soc-manufacturers/rockchip/rk3576/boards/seeed-recomputer-rk3576/) and adds more:
- 2.5 GbE instead of Gigabit Ethernet
- four USB 3.0 ports
- a second HDMI output and an HDMI input
- a second M.2 slot with PCIe 3.0 x4

It ships with Armbian and Seeed's reComputer AI Lab. The standard SKUs are the **RK3588-30** (8 GB) and **RK3588-40** (16 GB), and 32 GB is available to order. It launched in May 2026 from $199, and pre-orders shipped from 30 May 2026.
