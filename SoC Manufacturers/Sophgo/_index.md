# Sophgo

Sophgo (算能) is a Beijing chip designer that grew out of Bitmain's AI chip business. It makes Arm-based edge AI SoCs with its own TPU (BM1684X, BM1688), and RISC-V processors such as the SG2042 and the CV18xx camera SoCs.
