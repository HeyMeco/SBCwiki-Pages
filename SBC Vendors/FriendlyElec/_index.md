# FriendlyElec

![FriendlyElec Logo](assets/friendlyelec/FriendlyElec-Logo.png?h=90&f=webp)

FriendlyElec (Guangzhou FriendlyElec Technology) makes the NanoPi boards. Many of them are compact router boards running its own OpenWrt build, FriendlyWrt. Its vendor kernels tend to stay close to current Linux releases.

Website: <https://www.friendlyelec.com>
