# Libre Computer

![Libre Computer Logo](assets/librecomputer/LibreComputer-Logo.png?h=100&f=webp)

Libre Computer (Libre Computer Project) designs low-cost boards with Amlogic, Allwinner and Rockchip SoCs. Most of them follow the Raspberry Pi 3 footprints (CC and AC form factors) or the CM4 module format. The company maintains its boards in upstream Linux and U-Boot, ships UEFI firmware on the newer ones, and promises software support for 30 years from each board's launch.

Website: <https://libre.computer>
