# Arduino

![Arduino Logo](assets/arduino/Arduino-Logo.png?h=100&f=webp)

Arduino is an Italian open-source hardware company best known for its microcontroller boards. Its Linux-capable boards, the UNO Q and VENTUNO Q, pair a Qualcomm Dragonwing processor with an STM32 microcontroller on one board. Qualcomm announced its acquisition of Arduino in October 2025.

Website: <https://www.arduino.cc>
