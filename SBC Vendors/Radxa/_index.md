# Radxa

![Radxa Logo](assets/radxa/Radxa-Logo.png?h=100&f=webp)

Radxa is a Shenzhen single-board computer maker. Its lines include the ROCK and ZERO boards (mostly Rockchip and Amlogic), the Cubie boards (Allwinner), the Dragon boards (Qualcomm), the Orion boards (Cix) and the NIO boards (MediaTek).

Website: <https://radxa.com>
