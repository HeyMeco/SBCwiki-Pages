# Toradex

![Toradex Logo](assets/toradex/Toradex-Logo.png?h=100&f=webp)

Toradex is a Swiss embedded computing company best known for its NXP-based system-on-modules and the Torizon Linux platform. Its Luna single-board computers bring that software to production-ready boards, starting with the Synaptics Astra SL1680.

Website: <https://www.toradex.com>
