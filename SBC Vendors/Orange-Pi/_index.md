# Orange Pi

![Orange Pi Logo](assets/orangepi/OrangePi-Logo.png?h=100&f=webp)

Orange Pi is the single-board computer brand of Shenzhen Xunlong Software Co., Ltd. Its range includes low-cost Rockchip boards like the Orange Pi 5, the Cix P1-based Orange Pi 6 Plus and the Sophgo BM1688-based Orange Pi O1. It is also a launch partner for Cix's ClawCore chips.

Website: <http://www.orangepi.org>
